# Validation plan for the 500 g U4+M2 front

**Proposed on 18 November 2026.** The first decision is whether a guide can locate its rope holes while allowing the four mounting ribs to rotate under load. Start with M5 at the two stations 12 mm either side of its centre. The figures below come from published simulations through `c458329`. The numbered sections preserve the proposed test methods and decision criteria. Test 1 now has a separate execution report. [Reviewed revision](https://github.com/KevinQianjin/ASRC_snake_body_design/commit/c4583298a972e054d77cc4ee582e2121c5818cde)

Use the 500 g head including its adapter, four U modules followed by two M modules, 96 mm per module, 21/24/28 mm rope lane radii, 2 mm ropes, and M guide stations at -24, -12, +12 and +24 mm. Use the published central 1.8 mm NiTi backbone arrangement without an end ferrule. [Guide CAD](../cad/lanes_u4m2/guides_v3/README.md) · [End and anchor CAD](../cad/lanes_u4m2/end_bellmouth_v1/README.md)

## 1. Measure the effect of large rotation in simulation

The 18 November execution report gives the simulated moment-curvature response, individual land reactions, seat travel, strain locations and numerical refinement checks. The original protocol follows below. [Test 1 results](../reports/daily/M5_Joint_Test_2026_11_18.md) · [Case files, inputs and commands](../simulations/u4m2/lanes_20261113/m5_large_rotation_20261118/README.md)

Start with the archived M5 rib geometry and the same material, end restraints and loading axes as the linear model. Compare the bare PA12 body, the rigidly bonded guide and an ideal seat that locates the three translations and allows rotation. Apply pure bending incrementally to the record-146 curvature of 0.01749 rad/mm about the 0 and 67.5 degree axes. Sample the unloaded state and 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% of that curvature. Use a geometrically nonlinear formulation and recover the linear small-bend response first. [Linear model and inputs](../simulations/u4m2/lanes_20261113/evening_20261117/rib_joint_3d/rib_joint_3d_results.json)

Record the moment-curvature curve, tangent bending stiffness, joint force and moment, relative rotation, socket travel and peak rib strain with its location. Estimate torsional stiffness with a small twist at the same bend. Repeat the critical increment with a smaller load step and a finer local mesh. Proposed numerical checks are agreement within 5% for bending stiffness and appreciable peak reactions, and within 0.5 degrees for seat rotation. Report absolute differences for near-zero reactions. Carry a joint forward if its lower bending restraint persists beyond the refinement differences. If the ranking changes, refine that case first. Compare strain with data for the specified printed PA12 process and orientation. Keep strength open where material data or local strain convergence are missing.

Then prescribe the saved end-disk poses for each loaded comparison and apply that record's rope loads to the mounted guides. Recover the disk reactions. This tests joint loads at a known pose before the whole-arm solver predicts how the pose changes. The largest curvature, station force and station moment occur in different records. The values in this table are paired magnitudes at a single station.

| Saved record | M5 station | Station force | Simultaneous moment | Reason to test |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 146, left return | +12 mm | 27.54 N | 25.95 N·mm | Worst V3 guide overlap and maximum M5 curvature. Inspect both 12 mm stations. |
| 141, left excursion | +12 mm | 28.45 N | 22.04 N·mm | Highest resultant force in the station export. |
| 47, inverse hold | -12 mm | 11.11 N | 61.12 N·mm | Highest resultant moment in the station export. |
| 64, 175-degree inverse hold | +12 mm | 28.20 N | 18.12 N·mm | High-yaw routing and rod-mesh comparison. |

Use the signed per-hole force vectors at their bore locations and transform from the exported base frame into the joint model. Verify their resultant and moment against the station row with the same record, module and station. Applying that resultant again would double the rope load. Keep the pure-bending results as a separate reference for the effect of the attachment. ## 2. Check a retained guide on the real ribs

If the rotating seat retains its advantage, draw its bearing surfaces, retainer and assembly access on the actual webs. The linear idealisation required about 12.5 degrees of rotation and 0.92 mm of in-plane strut travel inside the socket. Size the candidate from the large-rotation result. Compare with V3 at 0.15 and 0.25 mm. Verify closed, watertight parts, axial location and nominal bore-centre invariance in the straight assembly. Under load, measure bore-centre displacement and check for binding, lift-off and loss of retention in both directions. [V3 geometry](../cad/lanes_u4m2/guides_v3/README.md) · [Bore comparison method](../simulations/u4m2/lanes_20261113/evening_20261116/rope_station_invariance.json)

Check the straight assembly and the four records above first. Extend the rib deformation model to M6 and the outer stations at ±24 mm before screening every saved hold against all eight guides (217 × 8). Record the minimum unintended clearance, part, hold and location. Check intended bearing surfaces separately for contact force and local strain. Confirm the worst and near-zero clearances with finer surfaces and solid-intersection checks. Advance a physical candidate when the retainer carries the paired loads and the minimum unintended clearance stays positive after numerical uncertainty and measured print/assembly tolerances. If production tolerances are unknown, record only the nominal geometry result and measure the parts in test 4.

## 3. Check the entire 2 mm rope at the ends and guides

Start with record 64 and the worst exit found in the archived per-rope data. Use the actual M5/M6 bellmouths, shared bores, anchor geometry and loaded guide positions. Allow lateral motion inside the shared bore and follow a continuous rope surface through the rigid-to-flexible transition. Compare both pulling directions with the frictionless reference. Feed the same route into rope length, tension, bearing loads and clearance. Repeat the 5,208 baseline exit checks across the 217 holds and list the additional guide-mouth and shared-bore checks separately. [Exit geometry and forces](../simulations/u4m2/lanes_20261113/morning_20261116/rigid_end_audit/rope_exits.csv)

Require endpoint closure, force balance and zero unintended penetration beyond numerical uncertainty. Keep at least the existing 1 degree margin below the bellmouth design wrap. Report intended bearing locations, smallest material bridge and minimum clearance elsewhere. The archived 0.005 mm allowance covers mesh faceting at intended bearing. Check the bridge against the print process and the applied load before enlarging a bore. Use measured pull-through friction when available and identify any range used for sensitivity beforehand.

## 4. Compare one-module builds on the bench

Begin with matched M5 bodies from the same PA12 print lot and orientation. Compare a bare rib body with the mounted candidate in the same end-disk bending fixture. Repeat the comparison with the specified backbone fitted to both assemblies. Record as-built gaps, rope-hole positions, rod fit and mass. Apply load and unload in steps within the measured material and fixture limits. Use a force or torque transducer and calibrated optical tracking of the ribs, guide holes and end disks. The first physical outputs are bending and torsional stiffness, seat rotation, residual slip and the smallest gap. Three matched pairs are a proposed starting sample for repeatability. [Existing module test methods](../simulations/u4m2/backbone_20261108/M_BACKBONE_TEST_PLAN.md)

Reproduce the simultaneous station loads through the actual ropes. Measure tension on both sides and winch travel on each pull and return. Inspect the retainer, bore walls and rope after cycling. Record force reversal, backlash, NiTi loading/unloading hysteresis, wear, cracks and any residual deformation above instrument repeatability. Compare one held-out specimen with the prediction using the measured stiffness and friction range. Record whether differences exceed combined measurement and model uncertainty. Define the service cycle count together with the intended duty before making a fatigue-life claim. A detached retainer, cracked rib or permanent guide shift calls for a joint revision before an assembly trial.

## 5. Replay the 500 g cycle and verify it on hardware

Use the selected CAD, mass, stiffness, loaded bore positions and rope path in one model. First compare identical reconstructed lengths at the critical holds with 8 and 16 rod cells per module. Include record 64. The historical 0.359 degree elevation change used fixed tensions. The later five-hold comparison used fixed lengths. Preserve both boundary conditions when interpreting mesh sensitivity. Then run loaded startup, straightening, insertion, right turn to +175 degrees and return, left turn to -175 degrees and return, and withdrawal at the 20 degree installation angle. Repeat with the measured stiffness, friction and backlash ranges. [Mesh comparisons](../reports/daily/Morning_Report_2026_11_16.md) · [Reference cycle and saved channels](../simulations/u4m2/lanes_20261113/GUIDED_VALIDATION.md)

Save every command and feedback substep with all 12 rope lengths and tensions, head pose, guide loads, minimum tank and internal clearances, node travel and the fixed-winch tangent. Use the existing numerical criteria for controlled motion, including at least 2.5 N operating tension, the 250 N diagnostic ceiling, no more than 1 degree yaw or elevation error and at least 5 mm tank clearance after the 1.5 mm allowance. Score the initial state separately. A simulated pass requires every commanded phase to complete and all motion and settling checks to pass. List missed or failed states by phase. The controller uses 0.2 degrees for numerical settling. Physical accuracy and dynamic stability require a corresponding trial with measured actuator response, sensor delay, the actual 500 g head and routed harness. Compare time histories, including reversals, against the simulation. [Controller settings](../simulations/u4m2/lanes_20261113/guided_validation_20261114/candidate/closed_loop/nominal_summary.json)

For each executed test, save the source commit, CAD and input hashes, boundary conditions, load frame, case or specimen ID, units, raw traces, refinement or measurement uncertainty and failures in a new dated evidence folder. Record the supported decision and the cases still open so the next run can resume from the first unresolved test.
